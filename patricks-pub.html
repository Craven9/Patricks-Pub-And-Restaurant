Our story

A real neighborhood bar in the heart of Croydon

Patrick's Pub & Restaurant has been a fixture on Bristol Pike for years. We're not a chain, not a franchise — just a place where the beer is cold, the food is honest, and everybody knows your name by the second visit.

Whether you're stopping in after work, watching the Eagles game, or celebrating something special, our team is here to make sure it's a night worth coming back for.
