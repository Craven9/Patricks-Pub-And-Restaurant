Our story

A real neighborhood bar in the heart of Croydon

Patrick's Pub & Restaurant has been a fixture on Bristol Pike for years. We're not a chain, not a franchise — just a place where the beer is cold, the prices are fair, and the staff treats every new face like a regular.

Come in for a tasty lunch, stay for good conversation. Shoot pool, throw some darts, or grab a take-out order on the way home. Whatever brings you through the door, you'll want to come back.
